Thumbi Taal

Dance in rhythm around Maveli's lamp
A dancer 💃 circles the lamp non-stop. Tap anywhere on the circle (or press Space) the instant she sweeps through the glowing golden arc at the top. Land it dead-centre for a Perfect, catch the edge for a Good — miss the arc entirely, or tap too early or late, and you lose a heart. The dance quickens with every beat you land — how long can you keep the taal?
